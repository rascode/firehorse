Spacing by relationship

Space says how things relate. A component never picks a number; it picks a relationship, and the relationship has one size everywhere. That is why a figure and its source line sit 4px apart on every tile, in every product.

The six steps

Gaps between things. Each is a token in the semantic layer, mapped onto the spacing ramp, so themes and data-fh-density scale every relationship together.

--fh-gap-bound
4px · Parts of one fact. A figure and its source line, a label and its value, an icon and its text.
--fh-gap-related
8px · Things that belong together. A figure and its chart or bar, chips in a row, a field label and its input.
--fh-gap-grouped
12px · Siblings. Tiles in a grid, rows in a list, fields in a form.
--fh-gap-grouped-wide
16px · Siblings that need air. Cards in a column, form rows.
--fh-gap-sectioned
24px · Parts of a card or panel. Head, body and foot of a section card; card padding.
--fh-gap-separated
48px · Sections on a page. One section card to the next, page bands.

Insets

Padding follows the same steps, named for what it pads.

--fh-inset-tight
8px · Chips, badges, small controls
--fh-inset-control
12px · Inputs, buttons, table cells across
--fh-inset-tile
16px · Tiles and inset cards
--fh-inset-card
24px · Cards, panels, section cards
--fh-inset-page
page margin · The page edge

The rules

Six rules, each with the reason behind it.

  1. Inside is tighter than outside. The space inside a thing is smaller than the space around it. That is how the eye knows where one thing ends.
  2. Padding is at least the gap. A container's padding is at least as large as the gap between its children, or the children look like they spill out.
  3. A rule replaces space. An etched rule stands in for a gap; it never adds one. Two rules in a row, or a rule plus a gap, reads as a mistake.
  4. One gap per relationship. Space comes from the parent's gap, not from stacked margins, so it can't double up.
  5. Numbers line up. Tiles in a row share rows, so every figure sits at the same height whatever the tile carries.
  6. No odd steps. 2px, 6px, 10px, 14px, 20px and 28px are not relationships. Each moves to the nearest step, or the reason is written beside it.

Tile anatomy

Every tile has the same rows, in the same order, so a row of tiles reads as one: title, description, gap, figure, chart or bar or people, source, detail. Title to description is bound; the gap is --fh-metric-gap; figure to chart is related; source is bound to what is above it.

Held by lint

npm run lint flags any raw --fh-space-* in a component and prints the count. It warns while the system moves over and becomes an error once the count reaches zero (--strict-spacing turns it on early). Tokens, themes and the layout primitives are exempt.
