    Design system from a URL

    The builder skill, below.

    Pro skill · firehorse-builder

    Hand it a URL. Get a design system back.

    The builder is an agent skill. Give it one reference site and it reads the sitemap, visits the key pages, screenshots them at three widths in light and dark, measures the real colors, type, spacing, radii and shadows, maps them onto Firehorse roles, checks contrast, and writes a theme, a config and a preview page you can open.

    1. Crawl

      robots.txt and sitemap.xml, then a sample of each page type.

    2. Capture

      Screenshots at 390, 768 and 1440 px; computed styles from the live DOM.

    3. Extract

      Clusters colors, type scale, spacing, radii and shadows into tokens.

    4. Map

      Fits the tokens to Firehorse roles, tones and config presets.

    5. Verify & ship

      Runs the contrast and schema checks, then writes theme, config and a preview.

    What counts as a project?

    One site or app on one production domain. Staging and local development are included and don't count.

    What's a hosted call?

    One request from a Pro field to a model through our key, such as one enrichment or one profile search. On your own key there's no allowance to track.

    Is the builder skill a one-off?

    No. Run it as often as you like on any plan with Pro, for your own sites or for clients.

    The ask

    A regional outdoor retailer with 14 stores is relaunching its site and app. Today: three codebases, two button styles, product cards built five different ways and a checkout that fails contrast. They want one system their in-house team and their AI agents can build on.

    Goals

    • One design system across web, app and in-store kiosks
    • WCAG 2.2 AA on every page, checked in CI
    • Agents can generate on-brand pages without review rounds
    • Seasonal re-skins without engineering tickets

    Scope

    Audit
    40 key screens, 3 codebases, analytics review
    Tokens
    Brand theme on Firehorse, light and dark, 9 tones mapped to departments
    Config
    Global presets: avatar, card, button, density; seasonal theme switch
    Components
    Store, cart, checkout, store locator, loyalty card, product datatable for staff
    Agents
    llms.txt, agent contract and schema rules for their own agents

    Deliverables

    • @harborpine/design package and theme file
    • Documentation site in the style of this one
    • 24 page templates in the playground
    • Migration plan with a ranked backlog
    • Two training sessions for designers and engineers

    Milestones

    1. Week 1–2

      Audit, token extraction with the builder, direction review

    2. Week 3–5

      Theme, config presets and core components

    3. Week 6–7

      Commerce and staff datatables, accessibility pass

    4. Week 8

      Docs, agent files, handover and training

    Not included

    • Rebuilding their e-commerce backend
    • Photography and copywriting
