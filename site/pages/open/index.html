W–Y Habit cards

Habit cards, drawn from Explore

W Explore tile

The Explore tile as it is: the area's cube, a mono eyebrow, the habit set large, one muted line. The arrow becomes an etched check; a hairline along the foot fills with the area's tone.

X Count cube

The cube carries the target, like the numbered cubes in Principles. The eyebrow reads the progress the way Explore reads families; the week sits underneath as seven letters.

Y Index row

Denser: one row per habit, three across. Cube, name, a tabular fraction and the check, with the same hairline at the foot. Nine habits in three rows.
