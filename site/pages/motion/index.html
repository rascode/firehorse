Motion contract

Most design systems stop at motion. Firehorse doesn't.

Copy a site and you get its boxes, not its timing. Agents left to guess motion guess differently every time: janky intros, inconsistent flows. Here every motion has a name, an Index ID and a rule, written down for people and for agents.

Durations: instant 80ms · fast 150ms · base 220ms · slow 420ms · enter 600ms · reveal 720ms · intro 900ms · scene 1.1s. Beat 150ms · stagger 60ms · rise 0.6rem · recede to 0.9.

Rules

The same seven hold for every piece; the agent contract carries them.

  1. Reduced motion wins. Everything appears in place; scroll pieces hold still; Drift shows one still frame.
  2. Content is never parked. Without script nothing is hidden; intros run only with fh-js and fh-ready arrives within 2s.
  3. Transform and opacity only. Clips and masks for draws and wipes; never width, height, top, left or margins.
  4. Tokens only. A new timing becomes a token first.
  5. One owner per sequence. The order lives on its section.
  6. Scroll-linked on the compositor. Script measures once and on resize, never per frame.
  7. Budget. No live blur on large or fixed surfaces; loops pause off screen; Drift runs at about 30fps.
