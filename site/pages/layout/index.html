02 Columns

Twelve columns, sixteen when you need them

For placing text and images off the obvious lines: offsets, asymmetric splits, overlaps. Children say where they sit with data-span and data-start. Like everything else here it follows its container: under 40rem it stacks, from 40rem data-span-md applies, from 60rem the full placement. The thinking behind it is the Rascode SDF grid policy.
