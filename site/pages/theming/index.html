02 Themes

Pick a horse. The whole site changes.

A theme is every design decision in one file: type, color in light and dark, corner radius, density, and how avatars, cards, buttons, chips and tables look. Apply it with one attribute, data-fh-theme, and switch it with a dropdown. Firehorse ships three to start. Build your own on top of Firehorse the same way.

Theme select

A native select. firehorse.js sets data-fh-theme on <html> and remembers it.

# compile it (checks AA contrast in both schemes)
npx firehorse-theme themes/appaloosa.json
# then
<link rel="stylesheet" href="themes/appaloosa.css">
<html data-fh-theme="appaloosa">
